# Round 02 — Cloudlets with fine granular detail

Alex selected “Combine Cloudlets + fine granular detail” in the desktop visual
review on 10 November 2026. This selects an appearance direction to develop,
not a final design, live implementation or accepted motion vocabulary.

This still study combines the softer pockets of Round 01 Cloudlets with a finer
layer of individual lights from Granular Light. The original Round 01 sheets are
unchanged. Attention, focused work and holding a concern use the same compositions
as before so the change in material can be judged fairly.

Open `hybrid.png` or `hybrid.svg`. There are no connecting lines, animation,
network calls or live data. Amber remains a proposed concern signal.

## Review question

Does this balance of soft groupings and individual lights feel right, or should
it be clearer, denser, softer or brighter? Does the concern remain contained
without becoming an angry face? Movement and laptop-size comfort remain untested.

## Reproduce

`make-study.mjs` is an artwork-authoring tool, not a product renderer. It combines
the existing original SVG layers; it does not alter Round 01. Node alone produces
SVG with `node make-study.mjs --svg-only`. For PNG, use an existing `sharp`
installation, or set `BRIAR_SHARP_MODULE` to its absolute module directory.
