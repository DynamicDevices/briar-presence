# Iteration plan

Work one reviewable question at a time. Keep accepted versions; change the design
when evidence or Alex's feedback warrants it. No automatic background work.

| Stage | Deliverable | Review question | Advance when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Brief | Requirements, proposals and boundaries | Is this the intended purpose and scope? | Alex confirms or corrects it |
| 2. Appearance | Three still directions, each with attention, focus and concern | Which light material and grouping feels right? | A direction or specific combination is selected |
| 3. Movement | Short clips plus reduced-motion equivalents | Are transitions expressive, calm and distinguishable? | Alex accepts a motion direction; ambiguities are recorded |
| 4. Multiple work | Simulated face and named-work navigation | Can someone find the affected work and needed action? | Navigation handles representative cases without relying on colour memory |
| 5. Laptop experience | Disposable manual prototype at plausible small sizes | Is it useful without distracting or obstructing? | Placement, visibility and attention behaviour are accepted |
| 6. Comprehension | Guided review with Alex; unfamiliar participants only with agreement | What do people actually think it means? | Misreadings lead to revisions, not claims of intuition |
| 7. Integration design | Observable-event contract, freshness rules and boundaries | Can each displayed state be truthful? | One narrowly scoped integration is agreed |
| 8. Implementation | Production work scoped separately | Does the real laptop result meet the agreed design? | Real acceptance checks, not a running demo |

Desk hardware and VR stay future design considerations. They do not block the
laptop study and are not authorised implementation milestones.

## Round 01: appearance, not animation

Compare three original directions on the same smoked-glass face:

- **A / Cloudlets:** soft, overlapping luminous pockets with small embedded lights.
- **B / Constellations:** discrete, legible groups of individual lights with dark gaps.
- **C / Granular light:** dense fine particles and irregular luminous formations.

Each shows attention, focused work and a local amber concern. These labels state
design intent, not demonstrated recognition. Avoid drawn connections, distinct
eyes, actual project names and live status. Review density, depth, brightness,
character and concern placement before judging motion.

## Round 02, after appearance selection

Animate a short simulated exchange: attention → exploration → focused work →
correction/reorganisation → concern held → waiting → resolution. Separately
show escalation to urgent and loss of connection. Do not imply every exchange
follows this sequence or that voiced worries necessarily resolve.

## Multiple-work test cases

Use fictional names only. Include a focused task with a concern elsewhere;
several concerns of different urgency; one awaiting the user; one awaiting an
external party; unknown/stale information; and a serious concern surviving a
switch of focus. Increase work-item count to expose clutter before choosing a
scaling rule. No speculative percentage-complete indicators.

## Review discipline

Record the question, version, concrete feedback, accepted changes, remaining
uncertainty and next experiment in REVIEW.md. Use DECISIONS.md for settled
requirements; do not mark proposals accepted through silence. Keep design
history independent of prototype code. No running poller, schedule or persistent
goal is created by this plan.
